Our Story

Who We Are

A Taste of Arabia in Kanjirappally

Meshwi Food Hub was created around one idea: bring the warmth and generosity of Arabian hospitality to Kanjirappally's dining scene. From aromatic slow-cooked Mandi to flame-grilled Alfaham and freshly rolled Shawarma, every dish is prepared with attention to authentic technique and fresh ingredients.

Our dining room is designed to feel calm and welcoming — whether you're bringing the whole family, catching up with friends, or stopping in during a trip through Kanjirappally.
