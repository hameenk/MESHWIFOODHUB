Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 — this is a placeholder policy. Please have this reviewed by a qualified professional before publishing.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our reservation form, newsletter sign-up, or contact us via WhatsApp or phone, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, and any details you choose to share (such as special requests or preferred dining times).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to reservation requests, confirm table availability, respond to enquiries, and — only if you opt in — send updates about new dishes, menus, or events.

3. WhatsApp & Phone Communication

Reservation and order requests submitted through this website may be forwarded to our team via WhatsApp using the details you provide. Standard WhatsApp terms and privacy practices apply to messages sent through that platform.

4. Newsletter & Marketing Consent

We only send marketing updates to people who have explicitly opted in via the consent checkbox on our newsletter form. You may unsubscribe at any time by contacting us directly.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Google Maps, Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager, or Meta Pixel to operate this website and understand how it is used. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. Analytics and tracking tools are only activated once appropriate consent mechanisms are in place.

6. Data Retention

We retain reservation and enquiry information only as long as necessary to fulfil your request and for reasonable record-keeping purposes.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below.

For any privacy-related questions, please contact Meshwi Food Hub at +91 80863 78686 or via our official social media channels.
